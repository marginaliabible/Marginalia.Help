# Translations panel

The **Translations** side panel shows the same verse in every translation you have selected, and lets you build a parallel Bible from starred ones.

[Show me how](tour:use-translations-panel)

The panel is accessed by clicking a verse number in the Bible, then choosing the **Translations** tab if not already active. The top of the panel displays the currently selected verse. Clicking it jumps back to that verse in your Bible.

## Selected translations

The [cards](target:translations-selected) at the top list each translation you have added for the current verse:

- Cards show the text of each selected translation.
- Use **×** to remove a translation from your selection. You cannot remove the last starred translation, otherwise you would have no Bible!
- Star a translation to include it in the parallel Bible columns.
- The text of verses in your starred translations are also used for [search](search) results.

## More Translations

A [list of links](target:translations-more) at the bottom of the panel lets you pick additional translations that match your language settings.

1. Type a search term in the [filter field](target:translations-filter) to narrow the list by name or even its abbreviation (for example `Segond` or `ESV`).
2. Click a translation name to add it. Newly selected translations are added as unstarred cards.

New languages can be added in [Settings](settings) to include additional translations for those languages. Additional translations can also be added under Account Sync → Subscriptions.

## Rearranging with drag and drop

Drag the **⋮** handle on a card header to reorder your selected translations. The order here will define the order of parallel translations in the Bible. Dragging a translation above/below starred/unstarred cards will change its star status.
