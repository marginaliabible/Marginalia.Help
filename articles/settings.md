# Settings

Settings control theme, preferred Bible languages, and where to manage library sources.

## Walk through Settings

[Show me how](tour:open-settings)

You can also open Settings with the gear in the header, then click the help icon next to Close for the same walkthrough.

## What you can change

- **Theme** — light or dark mode
- **Translator italics** — italicize words supplied by the translator*
- **Paragraph marks** — show paragraph markers*
- **Languages** — which languages appear when browsing translations
- **Sources** — libraries and subscriptions live under File Sync → Subscriptions

Close the dialog when you are done; your choices are remembered for the next visit.

*Available for supported translations such as KJV.
