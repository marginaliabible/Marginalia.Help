# Getting started

> [II Timothy 2:15](/#/II%20Timothy/2/15)  
> Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

Marginalia has over 160 translations of the Bible in over 60 languages all wrapped in a modern, easy to use interface with an unlimited number of parallel Bibles. But Marginalia is unique in that it brings your own notes and your ministry's resources directly into your Bible!

## Open a Bible

When the app loads, the King James Version is shown by default. Other translations from your browser's language are available as well. Open the [Translations panel](translations) to explore other translations in your language, or use [Settings](settings) to choose additional languages.

## Toolbar controls
There will be a visual indication of the currently selected verse or verses. From the toolbar, you can:
- Copy the text of the verse to the clipboard by clicking the **Copy** button or pressing `Ctrl+C`.
- Copy the URL to the selected verse by clicking the **Share** button.

## Verse selection
Multiple verses can be selected in multiple ways:
- On mobile, long-tap on a verse, then tap on another verse to select a range.
- With a verse selected, `Shift+Click` another verse to select a range of verses, or `Ctrl` to select multiple individual verses.
- Use `Shift+Up` or `Shift+Down` to select a range of verses.
- Use `Ctrl+Up` or `Ctrl+Down` to move your selection to the previous or next verse.

## Explore a verse
Click a verse number (or select verse text) to open [Translations](translations), **Resources**, and **Links** for that passage in the side panel.
