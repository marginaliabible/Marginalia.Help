# Document Viewer

The document viewer opens a resource at a specific page from the [Resources panel](resources), highlighting scripture references in the document.

## Toolbar

The toolbar at the top of the document viewer provides:
- The document title and page number
- Display/controls for [Zoom](#zoom)
- <i class="fa-solid fa-arrows-rotate" aria-hidden="true"></i> *Find in Account Sync* - Opens the [Account Sync](account-sync) panel to find this document in your synced resources. From there you can do things like removing the document from your synced resources or modifying its tags.
- <i class="fa-solid fa-cloud" aria-hidden="true"></i> / <i class="fa-brands fa-google-drive" aria-hidden="true"></i> *Open in cloud* - For documents synced from your cloud provider, opens the original file in OneDrive (<i class="fa-solid fa-cloud" aria-hidden="true"></i>) or Google Drive (<i class="fa-brands fa-google-drive" aria-hidden="true"></i>).
- <i class="fa-solid fa-up-right-from-square" aria-hidden="true"></i> *Pop into new browser window* (desktop only) - Opens the document in a new browser window. See [Popout Windows](popout-windows) for more details.
- *Back To Verse* - Closes the document viewer and returns to the Bible.

### Zoom
The current zoom level is displayed in the toolbar. By default, document zooms to fit the available width. Resizing the document viewer will adjust the zoom automatically. When auto-zoom is enabled, the current zoom level display shows a <i class="fa-solid fa-arrows-left-right" aria-hidden="true"></i> icon. 

Zoom in and out buttons are also available on either side of the zoom level display. Clicking these will adjust the zoom level by 20% increments. Clicking the zoom level will reset to back to auto-zoom.

## Document Frame
The document frame is the area of the document viewer that displays the document. It is the area that is scrollable. As the page is scrolled, the page number in the toolbar is updated to reflect the current page. Links to a document from the [Resources panel](resources) open to the specific page on which a reference occurred, and highlights the scripture reference in the document. Verses in the document are given a dashed underline. When clicked, the verses will open in the [Translations panel](translations).
